About Me

Where analytics meets purpose

Add your photo here

Save as assets/profile.jpeg

Open to remote, hybrid, freelance, and project-based opportunities.

I am a data analyst with a certification in Data Analytics and hands-on expertise in Power BI, Excel, Power Query, SQL, and Python. I build dashboards, clean messy datasets, and tell stories with numbers that support education, policy, and nonprofit work. Every spreadsheet, to me, is a window into real human decisions and community needs.

Previously, I earned my Master of Arts in History at the State University of New York at Geneseo, where my graduate thesis explored global power structures and economic diplomacy in a study of the semiconductor sector in the US China trade war from 2018 to 2025. Before that, I earned a Bachelor of Arts in History and International Studies from Ajayi Crowther University in Nigeria, with an undergraduate thesis examining Nigeria United States trade relations and the African Growth and Opportunity Act from 2015 to 2022.

I bring the same analytical rigor from historical research into data work, combining strong skills in research, writing, project management, mediation, and cross-cultural communication. I am deeply committed to community engagement and have volunteered at SUNY Geneseo's GREAT Day, multiple Diversity Summits, Livingston County Cares' Gumbo to Go, and the Rochester Aid Memorial Garden, where I helped clean and plant new bulbs.

My work continues to investigate how nations negotiate, collaborate, and compete within an increasingly complex international landscape, and how data can illuminate the path forward.
